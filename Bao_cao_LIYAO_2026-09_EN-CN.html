How to turn price into a strength:
  • Publish volume tiers (e.g. $0.02 at 1,000 pcs → $0.014–0.016 at 10,000–50,000 pcs). Listings that only show a single flat price look expensive next to tiered competitors.

  • Lead with MOQ, not price, in titles and images (sample / 1-set trial orders) — this is where the store genuinely beats most Chinese listings.

2.4 Verdict & how to turn price into an advantage (incl. Vietnamese peers)

Answer to the question “is price a strength?” — it depends on who you compare against.
  • Versus Chinese suppliers — no: the store prices at the market median ($0.02 insert nut against a $0.01–0.03 cluster; barrel nut $0.02–0.05 against $0.012–0.101), and several Chinese sellers reach $0.01–0.025 at 10,000–100,000 pcs — tiers the store does not currently publish.
  • Versus Vietnamese peers, especially Binh Duong — yes, at low-to-mid MOQ: against ZHIJIA VIETNAM (same city) LIYAO is roughly 33–60% cheaper on insert nuts and about 50% cheaper on barrel nuts at the same 1,000-pc MOQ. At 100,000+ pcs, however, ZHIJIA's published $0.01–0.03 is lower.
  • Overall: price is a moderate relative strength domestically, but not a decisive one. The durable advantages remain MOQ flexibility (1-set tube insert nuts), Vietnam origin and lead time — and on service, ZHIJIA currently leads (≤2h / 100% vs ≤4h / 95.45%).
  • What is a strength: MOQ flexibility (tube insert nuts at 1 set; most items at 1,000 pcs), Vietnam origin (EU/Vietnam trade preferences, non-China sourcing, shorter lead times to Europe), and service speed (0.76h response — faster than the industry leaders).
  • Publish tiered pricing: add a 10,000 / 50,000-pc tier (e.g. $0.014–0.016 for insert nuts). Flat single-price listings lose to tiered ones in buyer comparisons and in the price filter.
  • Shift the comparison to a kit: sell cam-lock + dowel + bolt assemblies or boxed furniture-fixing sets so buyers compare total assembly cost rather than unit price.
  • Lead with MOQ and origin in the title and images: “1-set sample available · Made in Vietnam” is a differentiator Chinese suppliers cannot match.
  • Fix the traffic problem first: with only 916 impressions in September, even the sharpest pricing cannot generate orders — the October priority is listings (300+), Top/Super products, RTS exposure and a small KWA budget to add the 7 visitors needed for 1★.

2.4 结论与「把价格变成优势」的建议（含越南同行）

回答「价格是否是优势？」— 取决于和谁比。
  • 对比中国供应商 — 不是优势：本店处于市场中位（预埋螺母 $0.02，竞品集中在 $0.01–0.03；横孔螺母 $0.02–0.05，竞品 $0.012–0.101）。多家中国供应商在 10,000–100,000 件量级可给到 $0.01–0.025 — 而本店尚未公布这些阶梯。
  • 对比越南同行（尤其平阳省）— 在中低起订量下是优势：与同城的 ZHIJIA VIETNAM 相比，同样 1,000 件起订，本店预埋螺母低约 33–60%、横孔螺母低约 50%。但在 10 万件以上，ZHIJIA 公布的 $0.01–0.03 更低。
  • 总体：价格在越南国内属中等偏强的相对优势，但不是决定性优势。真正的长期优势仍是起订量灵活（钢管内置螺母 1 套起订）、越南产地与交期 — 而服务速度目前由 ZHIJIA 领先（≤2h / 100% vs ≤4h / 95.45%）。
  • 真正的优势在于：起订量灵活（钢管内置螺母 1 套起订，其余 1,000 件）、越南产地（欧盟/越南贸易优惠、非中国供应链、发往欧洲交期更短）、以及服务速度（0.76 小时，快于行业领先）。
  • 公布阶梯价格：增加 10,000 / 50,000 件档（例如预埋螺母 $0.014–0.016）。只显示单一价格的链接，在买家比价与价格筛选中容易吃亏。
  • 把比较维度转向"套件"：销售三合一连接件 + 偏心轮 + 螺栓组合，或整套家具装配包，让买家比较整装成本而非单价。
  • 标题与主图主打起订量与产地："1 套样品可订 · Made in Vietnam" 是中国供应商无法复制的差异点。
  • 先解决流量问题：9月仅 916 次曝光，价格再优也难以成单 — 十月优先做商品数（300+）、Top/Super 商品、RTS 曝光，并用小额 KWA 补足达到 1★ 所需的 7 位访客。
